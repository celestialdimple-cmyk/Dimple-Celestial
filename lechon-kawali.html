Lechon Kawali Recipe

A Filipino dish made with crispy, deep-fried pork belly.

Details of Letchon kawali

Category Information
Preparation Time 10 to 15 minutes
Cooking Time 30 to 40 minutes
Total Time 40–55 minutes
Serving Size 4 people ore more

About the Dish

Lechon Kawali is a popular Filipino dish known for its crispy skin and tender, juicy meat. It is usually served with rice and lechon sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram pork belly
  • 6 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns
  • 3 dried bay leaves
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • Cooking oil for deep-frying
  • Lechon sauce for serving

Instructions

  1. Place the pork belly in a large cooking pot.
  2. Add water, salt, peppercorns, bay leaves, and garlic.
  3. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 45 to 60 minutes.
  4. Remove the pork belly and allow it to cool.
  5. Pat the pork belly dry using paper towels.
  6. Heat enough cooking oil in a deep pot.
  7. Carefully fry the pork belly until the skin becomes golden brown and crispy.
  8. Remove it from the oil and let it rest before slicing.
  9. Serve with rice and lechon sauce.
