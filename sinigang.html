Details of Sinigang

Category Information
Preparation Time 15 minutes
Cooking Time 45–60 minutes
Total Time 60–75 minutes
Serving Size 4 people

About Sinigang

Sinigang is a popular Filipino dish known for its sour and delicious soup. It is usually cooked with pork, vegetables, and tamarind seasoning.

Ingredients

  • 500 grams pork
  • 1 packet tamarind soup mix
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 radish
  • 1 eggplant
  • 1 bunch kangkong
  • 6 cups water
  • Fish sauce

How to Cook Sinigang

  1. Boil the pork with water, onion, and tomatoes.
  2. Cook until the pork becomes tender.
  3. Add the radish and eggplant.
  4. Add the tamarind soup mix.
  5. Add the kangkong and cook for a few minutes.
  6. Add fish sauce according to your taste.
  7. Serve hot with steamed rice. Enjoy!

Why I Love Sinigang

Sinigang is one of my favorite Filipino dishes because of its sour and flavorful soup. It is best enjoyed with family and a warm plate of rice.
