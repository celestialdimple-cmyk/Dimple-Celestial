Details of the Chicken Adobo

Category Information
Preparation time 15 minutes>
Marinating time 30 minutes optional
Cooking time 40 to 45 minutes
Total time 55 to 60 minutes without marinating
Serving size 4 to 5 servings

Description

Chicken Adobo is one of the most beloved dishes in the Philippines. It features chicken braised in a savory and tangy sauce made from soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and bay leaves. It's simple to make and tastes even better the next day!

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces (thighs and drumsticks)
  • ½ cup soy sauce
  • ⅓ cup white vinegar
  • 1 whole head garlic, crushed
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • 3–4 dried bay leaves
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar (optional)

Instructions

  1. Combine chicken, soy sauce, and garlic in a bowl. Marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a large pan or pot over medium heat.
  3. Remove chicken from marinade, reserving the marinade. Pan-fry chicken for 2–3 minutes per side until lightly browned.
  4. Pour the reserved marinade into the pan. Add water, vinegar, peppercorns, and bay leaves. Do not stir.
  5. Bring to a boil, then lower heat to simmer. Cover and cook for 30–35 minutes until chicken is tender.
  6. Remove the lid and simmer for another 10 minutes until sauce thickens to your liking.
  7. Remove from heat. Serve hot over steamed rice. Enjoy!
